Privacy Policy

Last updated: [DATE]

The short version: we only ask for what we need to tell you when the kit is ready. We don’t sell your information, and you can unsubscribe or ask us to delete it at any time.

Who we are

The Rideshare Tax Kit is a product of Sharpline Emergence Ltd., a British Columbia company. We’re responsible for the personal information collected on this website. Questions about privacy go to our privacy contact at hello@sharplinedigital.com.

What we collect

When you reserve the kit, we collect:

  • your first name and email address
  • your province, and which platforms you drive for

We don’t ask for your SIN, GST/HST number, income, banking details or anything else about your taxes. This site doesn’t use advertising or tracking cookies.

When you visit, we also count the visit and note which link brought you here (for example, a post in a Facebook group). That count has no cookies and nothing that identifies you.

Why we collect it

  • To email you when the kit is ready, with your founding price.
  • To learn which provinces and platforms to support first.

We won’t use your information for anything else without asking you first.

Your consent, and how to withdraw it

We only email you if you tick the box on the reservation form. Every email we send includes an unsubscribe link, and you can also just reply asking to be removed. We’ll take you off the list within 10 business days.

Who else handles it

We never sell or rent your information. We use a few service providers to run this site, and they handle your information only on our behalf:

  • Formspree receives your reservation, stores it, emails it to us and filters out spam.
  • Supabase keeps our master copy of the reservation list in a secure database.
  • Hostinger Mail handles our email, including the launch email we send you.
  • GitHub Pages hosts this website and may keep standard server logs, such as your IP address.
  • Google Fonts supplies the text styles on this page, so your browser connects to Google when the page loads.

Some of these providers store or process information outside Canada, including in the United States. While it’s there, it may be accessible to courts and authorities under that country’s laws.

How long we keep it

Until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete it. If the kit hasn’t launched within 12 months of your reservation, we’ll delete the list.

How we protect it

The site uses an encrypted (HTTPS) connection. The reservation list is stored in a password-protected database that only we can access.

Your rights

You can ask to see the information we hold about you, correct it, or have it deleted. Email hello@sharplinedigital.com and we’ll respond within 30 days.

If you’re not satisfied with how we handle a privacy concern, you can contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada or, in BC, the Office of the Information and Privacy Commissioner for British Columbia.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle your information, we’ll update this page and the date at the top. Before we start taking payments, we’ll add details about our payment provider here.
